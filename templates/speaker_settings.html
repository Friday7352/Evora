Voice setup

Set up voices on the Evora computer. You can use this page from either PC on your home network.

Checking Evora…

1. Choose how to follow voices

Joint separation is a candidate to test, not a verified accuracy upgrade. It attempts up to three voices in each short model window. Four to six people speaking together can still remain mixed. Temporary “Voice” labels are estimates and are not accepted by strict speaker filters.

Standard mode options

2. Download the voice model

Hugging Face hosts the model files. Its free account gives Evora permission to download them. Your conversations are processed locally on your Evora PC.

  1. Sign in to Hugging Face and accept access on the model page below.
  2. If you already saved a read token in Evora, leave the token box blank. A fine-grained token must also allow access to the new models.
  3. Click Prepare voices. Evora downloads the files and checks that the model can run.

I do not have a token yet

Open Hugging Face tokens ↗, create a Read token, and paste it into the box above. Never share it in chat. Evora saves it only on the Evora computer and never returns it from this page.

3. Try a recording first

Stop listening in Frivo, then choose a recording of up to one minute (256 MB maximum). Audio or video works. This checks speed and lets you hear the separated voices before relying on them live.

The test does not change saved voices or live sessions. Preview audio is held temporarily in memory. A speed result is not an accuracy score: listen for the same person staying on the same label and other voices leaking into their audio.

Remember someone you know

Choose 20–30 seconds of that person speaking alone and enter their name. Named voices survive restarts. Temporary voices belong only to the current listening session.

    After changing speaker mode, stop and restart listening in Frivo.
